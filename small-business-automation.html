A workflow you can explore.

Imagine a shop receiving new stock. Staff need to turn rough notes into tidy listings, check the details, and export the approved items.

The phone below is interactive. Scroll inside it, tap the buttons, and edit a listing. On mobile, you can also open it full screen.

  1. Explore the intake notes. Tap “Show example listings.”
  2. Review the drafts. Continue to review and change a title or price.
  3. Choose what is ready. Export the approved examples to a CSV spreadsheet.

Fictional demo. Items, prices, and listing text are sample data. Editing, approval, and CSV export work in your browser. No live AI generation or store publishing runs here.
